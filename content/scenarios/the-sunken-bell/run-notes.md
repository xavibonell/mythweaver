# The Sunken Bell of Mistmoor — Run Notes (GM-facing)

A ~60–90 minute level-1 one-shot for **3 pre-gens** (Sir Aldric the Fighter, Brakka the Cleric, Pip the Rogue).
Built to exercise the v1 play loop: **social/exploration → skill checks → one combat**. Text-first.

> **Licensing:** Local concept-proof for private play — there is no SRD-only restriction (spec §11). The
> prose here is original to MythWeaver; the **Goblin** and **Giant Rat** stat blocks in `bestiary.json` are
> 5e-compatible. Provenance labels are kept for citation, not as an ingestion gate.

## The truth behind it

Mistmoor's warning bell didn't fall to the drowned dead. Three nights ago a small goblin band toppled it to
raid under cover of fog, tore out the heavy **iron clapper** (they'll sell it to a marsh-witch downriver), and
grabbed the fisher **Tomen** when he stumbled onto them. **Edda Marsh**, the old bellkeeper, quietly paid them
to leave and is too ashamed to admit it — hence her evasiveness.

## Beats

1. **Mistmoor Green (social).** Establish fog, fear, the dark tower. Edda is grateful but cagey. The goal is
   simply to point the party at the tower. **Offer three independent routes** so no party is stuck:
   - Insight DC 12 → "she's hiding something."
   - Persuasion DC 13 → she confesses the bargain and the goblins.
   - Investigation / Perception DC 12 → small clawed tracks toward the tower.
   Any **one** success is enough. Failing all three: a frightened child mentions "the green men in the tower."
2. **The Drowned Bell-Tower (exploration / skill).** Three redundant ways in (climb / sneak / barge). A
   Perception or Survival DC 12 spots the tin-can alarm on the stair. This scene rewards the Rogue but never
   *requires* stealth — the Fighter can climb, the Cleric can talk.
3. **The Undercroft (combat).** Two goblins + a giant rat (see scaling). Tomen is bound here, alive.

## Combat tactics

- Goblins open from cover with shortbows, then **Nimble Escape** (bonus-action Disengage/Hide) to skirmish.
- The giant rat has **Pack Tactics** — it piles onto whoever a goblin is already next to.
- Morale: when reduced to a single combatant, the survivor grabs the clapper and runs for the reeds.

## Dynamic balancing (spec Module B intent, previewed)

The critical path is solvable by **at least three different ability profiles** (might / stealth / charm), so a
party lacking any one is never hard-blocked — exactly the "shift skill gates to optional, keep the main path
open" rule. If you add a 4th PC, add **one more goblin**; for a 2-PC table, drop the giant rat.

## Difficulty math (3 PCs)

2 × Goblin (50 XP) + 1 × Giant Rat (25 XP) = 125 XP, ×2 encounter multiplier ≈ **250 adjusted XP** — a solid
*medium* fight for three level-1 heroes. Survivable, with real stakes if the goblins focus the Rogue.

## Rewards

- The recovered **iron clapper** (rehang it → the bell tolls → the denouement).
- A goblin coin-pouch: **15 gp** and a chipped river-pearl (10 gp).
- **1 potion of healing** stuffed in the goblins' loot sack.

## Read-aloud seeds (optional — the DM persona will riff on these)

- *Arrival:* "Mist sits on Mistmoor like a held breath. No bell. Only the slap of water on stilts, and the dark
  tilt of the tower across the fen."
- *Tower:* "The great bell lies face-down in the muck, a bronze moon fallen to earth — and where its iron tongue
  should hang, there's only a torn socket."
