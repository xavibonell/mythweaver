# Combat (SRD 5.1)

## Armor Class & attack rolls
To make an attack, roll **d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus** (Strength for most melee, Dexterity for ranged and finesse). If the total **equals or exceeds the target's Armor Class (AC)**, the attack hits.

- A **natural 20** on the d20 always hits and is a **critical hit** (roll the attack's damage dice twice).
- A **natural 1** always misses.

## Damage & healing
On a hit, roll the weapon/spell **damage dice + the ability modifier** used for the attack (a critical hit doubles the dice, not the modifier). Each source of damage has a **type** (e.g. slashing, fire, radiant).

- **Resistance:** take **half** damage of that type.
- **Vulnerability:** take **double** damage of that type.
- **Immunity:** take **no** damage of that type.

A creature drops to **0 hit points** when reduced to 0; excess damage does not carry over unless it equals the creature's HP maximum (instant death). Player characters at 0 HP make **death saving throws**.

## Initiative
At the start of combat, each participant rolls **initiative** (a Dexterity check). Turns proceed from highest to lowest.

## A turn's economy
On your turn you can **move** up to your speed and take **one action**. Some abilities grant a **bonus action**, and you get **one reaction** per round (e.g. an opportunity attack).

Common actions: **Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object.**

## Cover
- **Half cover:** +2 to AC and Dexterity saves.
- **Three-quarters cover:** +5 to AC and Dexterity saves.
- **Total cover:** can't be targeted directly.
