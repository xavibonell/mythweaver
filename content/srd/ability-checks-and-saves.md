# Ability Checks, Skills & Saving Throws (SRD 5.1)

## Ability checks
An ability check tests a creature's talent and training. Roll **d20 + the relevant ability modifier** (+ proficiency bonus if proficient in a relevant skill), and compare to a **Difficulty Class (DC)**. Equal to or greater than the DC succeeds.

## Typical DCs
| Task | DC |
|---|---|
| Very easy | 5 |
| Easy | 10 |
| Medium | 15 |
| Hard | 20 |
| Very hard | 25 |
| Nearly impossible | 30 |

## Skills by ability
- **Strength:** Athletics
- **Dexterity:** Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth
- **Intelligence:** Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion
- **Wisdom:** Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival
- **Charisma:** Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion

## Advantage & disadvantage
Roll **two d20s**; with advantage take the **higher**, with disadvantage take the **lower**. They don't stack, and one of each cancels out. (The range of possible totals is unchanged — only the odds shift.)

## Passive checks
A passive check is **10 + all modifiers**, +5 with advantage or −5 with disadvantage. Passive Perception is the common example.

## Contests & working together
When two creatures oppose each other, both make an ability check; higher wins (a tie means no change). One creature can **Help** another, granting advantage on the helped check.

## Saving throws
A saving throw resists a threat (a spell, trap, poison, etc.). Roll **d20 + the relevant ability modifier** (+ proficiency bonus if proficient in that save), versus a DC set by the effect.
